## Resolved

**The Accelerator is 15, not 16.** The 22 May launch post is authoritative: 36 of 40 eligible
administrations applied, 15 were selected by an independent multi-stakeholder technical
committee. The sixteenth grant in circulation is **Khomyn Tal takhi — an earmarked grant**,
not a competitive Accelerator award. These are different instruments and the issue must not
merge them: a donor reading "16 selected" would be reading a directed grant as a competitive
one. The Khomyn Tal earmarked grant currently has no home in the issue plan.

Source figures now used in the feature: up to **16.5 billion MNT** total; **500 million to
3 billion MNT** per administration; two phases (Readiness, Management Improvement); the 15
cover **over 40%** of the national protected area estate.

Still unconfirmed and therefore unwritten: the 15–40M MNT phase-one range, the 1 October
Readiness close, and any disbursement figure.
